# CBT Exam Fees Notification: B.Tech I Year I Sem (BH-25) Regular, February 2026
## Overview
Dated 28-01-2026, Examination Branch, BVRIT Hyderabad. The college offers a Computer Based Test (CBT) for B.Tech I Year I Sem (BH25) regular students in Feb 2026, for improvement of internal marks and for students absent in mid-term exam(s). CBT exams commence from 11-02-2026; registration opens 28-01-2026. Timetable to be notified later.
## Registration and fee
- Without late fee: 28-01-2026 to 03-02-2026
- With late fee Rs.100: 04-02-2026 to 05-02-2026
- With late fee Rs.1000: 06-02-2026 only
- Examination fee for CBT: Rs.1000/- per subject
- Mode of payment: OFFLINE (by cash)
## CBT format and eligibility
- 30 objective questions from the entire syllabus, answered offline, 30 minutes, 30 marks (1 mark each).
- Available per course for students who missed one of the two mid-term exams due to unavoidable circumstances, or attended both mid-terms but want to improve internal marks.
## Marking
CBT marks are treated as equivalent to one mid-term exam. Absent students in a mid-term get zero for it. The average of the best two scores from the three exams (two mid-terms and the CBT), combined with other internal assessment components, forms the Continuous Internal Improvement (CII) marks for that course.
