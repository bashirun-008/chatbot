# Higiene Biodegradable Sanitary Pad (Raksha Higiene Private Limited)
## About
Raksha Higiene Private Limited, tagline "Empowering Women, Protecting the Planet"; brand Higiene, "Comfort, Care, Confidence". Launching what it calls India's truly complete biodegradable sanitary napkin. The company says conventional pads contain chemicals such as phthalates and dioxins, and that so-called organic pads often use super absorbent polymers (SAPs) that take 250-500 years to degrade. (These are the company's own marketing claims.)
## Technology
Made in collaboration with IIT Delhi material research scientists, tested and validated by SITRA, using high-absorption natural gums.
## Why choose these pads (company claims)
Truly biodegradable (decompose within months); chemical-free (no phthalates, dioxins or bleaching agents); superior absorption through natural gum technology; scientifically tested by IIT Delhi scientists and SITRA; accessible and affordable.
