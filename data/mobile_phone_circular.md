# Circular on Mobile Phones, AirPods and Earphones, Cir. No. BVRITH/2025/47, 4 Sep 2025
## Scope
Applies to all students, faculty and staff, effective immediately. Subject: Prohibition on using mobile phones, AirPods and earphones in academic premises.
## Prohibited areas and times
Areas: classrooms, libraries, laboratories, lecture halls, study areas. Times: during classes, lectures and lab sessions, and while interacting with faculty, staff or peers in academic settings.
## Policy
- Mobile phones must be switched off or on silent and kept out of sight in designated areas.
- AirPods, earphones or any listening devices are strictly prohibited in academic premises, except for individuals with documented hearing impairments or special needs.
## Consequences
Repeated violations lead to warnings and disciplinary action, which may include confiscation of mobile or listening devices and action on disobeying rules.
## Exceptions
- Emergency: inform the faculty/staff in charge and seek permission to use the phone.
- Documented hearing impairment or special needs: contact the Vice Principal / respective HoD for reasonable accommodations.
