# Declaration Form: BH25 Regulations (BVRITHCEW-A)
## Purpose
Declaration signed by I Year B.Tech / M.Tech students (College Code WH) with parent/guardian signature(s), endorsed by Head of Department and Principal, with college stamp. The student declares awareness of the following.
## Points declared
1. Academic regulations prescribed by BVRITHCEW(A) from AY2025-26 onwards.
2. Course registration before commencement of each semester with help of faculty mentor/advisor/HoD.
3. Attendance detention procedure; minimum attendance requirement is 75% without condonation to be promoted to the next semester/year.
4. Credit detention regulations and minimum credits needed to be promoted to the next academic year.
5. Project guidelines (Real Time Research Project / Field Based Research Project / Mini / Major) as per BH25.
6. Minimum marks: in Semester End Examination (SEE) 35% of 60, i.e. 21 out of 60; and minimum 40% of total 100, i.e. 40 out of 100, with CIE and SEE taken together.
7. Guidelines for re-admission from one regulation to the readmitted year's regulations.
8. Malpractice rules and punishment.
9. Punishment for ragging, if involved.
