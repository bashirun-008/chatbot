# Circular on Student Affairs Council (SAC) Nominations, Cir. No. BVRITH/2025/46, 4 Sep 2025
## Call for nominations
BVRIT Hyderabad, Bachupally. Nominations / self-nominations for the Student Affairs Council (SAC) are invited via a Google form that will be shared. The SAC has representatives from various disciplines and year groups. Deadline to fill the form: 8 September 2025.
## SAC roles
1. President: Final Year
2. Vice President: Third Year
3. Treasurer: Third Year
4. Secretary: Second Year
5. Joint Secretary: First Year
6. 5 Student Members
## Departments and sections
Departments: EEE, ECE, CSE, AIML, IT; the circular includes a table of year-wise sections (I Year sections A-F, II to IV Year sections A-C) per department. Signed by Principal.
