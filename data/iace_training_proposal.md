# iACE Training Proposal for Shri Vishnu College of Engineering (Proposal No. iACE/BD/13092025, 13 Sept 2025)
## About iACE
International Automobile Centre of Excellence (iACE) (www.iace.ac.in) is an industry-first institute centre with state-of-the-art industry-ready labs and equipment for hands-on learning, built on industry-academia collaborations with Indian and foreign players. iACE is an Awarding Body recognized by NCVET; trainees completing NSQF-approved qualifications receive an NCVET certificate. Logos: Government of Gujarat, Maruti Suzuki.
## Objective and programs
A 10-day Industrial Training Program (ITP) giving students industry-relevant, hands-on training and exposure to real-world manufacturing. Three tracks, each 10 days: AI-ML, GD&T, HVAC.
## Track: AI-ML (10 days)
Automotive ecosystem overview (Toyota Fortuner demo); electronics with automotive perspective; digital electronics in automobile; communication in automobiles (CAN bus, LIN, using oscilloscope); ADAS; image processing and recognition; industrial robotics (pick-and-place, IR vision camera); micro-controllers and micro-processors; sensorics; Industry 4.0; one industrial visit day; one cultural tour day (Gujarat culture).
## Track: GD&T (10 days)
Machining technology: introduction to manufacturing technology (lathe, CNC, VMC, safety); metal cutting tools and techniques; metrology; part drawing, limits, fits and tolerances, GD&T; machining process planning; fundamentals of VMC programming; linear interpolation VMC/CNC; circular interpolation VMC/CNC; industrial visit day; cultural tour day (Gujarat culture).
## Track: HVAC (10 days)
Automotive ecosystem overview; electronics with automotive perspective; sensorics (inductive, Hall effect, piezoelectric, NTC/PTC, potentiometer); lighting systems; digital electronics; fundamentals of automotive HVAC; HVAC components and functions (compressor, condenser, evaporator, expansion valve, blower, etc.); diagnostics and troubleshooting (manifold gauges, leak detectors, refrigerant recovery, vacuum, recharging); industrial visit day; cultural tour day (Indian culture).
## Execution and resources
Vocational pedagogy with hands-on training and IST-accomplished trainers. Learners are evaluated on participation in theory sessions, hands-on training and industry visits. A product manager handles communication and reports. Resources: training content (digital, limited hardcopy), stationery, and food (breakfast, lunch, snacks).
## Cost proposal (valid 15 days from the proposal date)
- Training cost: Rs.1,500 per person per day
- Hostel (triple sharing): Rs.800 per person per day
- Total per student for the 10-day training: Rs.23,000
Terms: breakfast, lunch and evening snacks are included in training fees, served at iACE premises; dinner is included in hostel fees; prices are inclusive of taxes (tax applicability depends on the segment of training); price applies to a minimum batch of 15; prices are estimates and subject to change with scope.
