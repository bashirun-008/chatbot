# General Instructions to Parents and Students Regarding Examinations (dated 01-10-2026)
## Exam timings
- End Semester exams: FN 10:00 AM to 01:00 PM; AN 02:00 PM to 05:00 PM.
- Internal (Mid) exams: FN 10:30 AM to 12:30 PM; AN 02:00 PM to 04:00 PM.
## Instructions to parents
- Ensure the ward attends all exams and reaches the centre well before reporting time.
- Ensure all applicable exam fees are paid within the due date before appearing.
- Ensure adequate rest, food and sleep during the exam period.
- Approach the Examination Branch for exam queries instead of unofficial information.
- Health issues: inform the Examination Branch in advance; submit the request to the Chief Superintendent with supporting medical documents at least three days before the exams commence.
- Malpractice can lead to disciplinary action (per academic regulations booklet) including cancellation of all exams written in the semester; parents will be called to a college meeting.
## Dress code and items
- Students must carry valid college ID card and hall ticket/admit card.
- Dress code: Chudidar/Panjabi dress. Jeans, shirts with pockets and shoes are not permitted inside the exam hall.
- Mobile phones, tablets, electronic gadgets, communication devices, books, notes, papers and bits of paper are prohibited in the hall, even if switched off.
## Entry, exit and conduct in the hall
- Check hall number and seating plan daily.
- Enter at least 15 minutes before the exam starts. No entry after the exam commences.
- Leaving the hall is allowed only after one and a half hours from commencement. Early exit only in a genuine emergency with prior permission of the Controller of Examinations; for health issues a valid doctor's certificate is needed and the student must be assisted by supporting staff.
- No writing beyond the prescribed time. Maintain silence and discipline; follow invigilator instructions.
- Sign the attendance sheet before leaving.
## Answer booklet and question paper
- Check the answer book is undamaged and has all pages; no additional sheets are issued.
- Verify Register Number, Degree/Branch, Course code/Name and exam date on the booklet.
- Verify course code and subject name on the question paper; return an incorrect paper at once. Report missing data/figures to the invigilator.
- Rough work must be done in the answer book itself (it becomes part of the answer paper). Strike off all blank/unused pages after finishing.
- Do not detach sheets or smuggle out sheets/book: violation leads to cancellation of all exams written that semester.
- Write the Register number only in the specified space on the first page. Writing your name, appeals to the examiner, internal assessment marks or any identifying mark is treated as an attempt to influence the examiner and attracts disciplinary proceedings.
## Materials
- Bring your own pens, pencils, eraser, geometrical instruments and calculators; borrowing is not allowed.
- Only non-programmable calculators are permitted.
- Mathematical/statistical tables and handbooks are supplied on request and must be returned immediately without scribbling.
## Malpractice
Students indulging in malpractice are referred to the Controller of Examinations for disciplinary proceedings, which may include cancellation of all exams written during the semester.
